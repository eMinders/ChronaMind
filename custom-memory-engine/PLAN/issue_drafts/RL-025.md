# [RL-025] Benchmark adapters (`LongMemEval` and `LoCoMo`)

**Milestone:** `P2 Benchmarks and own suites`  
**Labels:** `enhancement`  
**Proposed Owner:** Member 4 (`R-DB`) · **Peer Reviewer:** Member 5 (`R-APP`)

## Objective & Why
Implement the dataset loaders, replay-clock preprocessors (`D-23`), evidence-mapping helpers (`Evidence Recall@k`), and official scorer wrappers for `LongMemEval` and `LoCoMo` (`Graduation_Project_Proposal.md` §§7.4, 10.2, 10.3).

## Scope & Deliverables
- **In Scope:**
  - `LongMemEval` adapter: loads `oracle`/`S`/`M`, stable-sorts sessions by timestamp (`Proposal` §7.4 rule `3`), sets `ρ = max(question_date, last_turn_ts)` (`D-23`), maps `has_answer` gold turns to extracted memories via `MEMORY_EVIDENCE` for `Evidence Recall@k` (excluding the 30 abstention and 56 single-session-assistant questions from the primary metric per `Proposal` §10.3), and wraps the official QA judge prompts.
  - `LoCoMo` adapter: loads `locomo10.json`, maps dialogue evidence IDs, and invokes the official token-F1 / partial-F1 / abstention scoring by numeric category (`1`–`5`) per conversation.
- **Out of Scope:**
  - Implementing baselines `B0`–`B4` (`RL-031`) or the LLM judge (`RL-033`).
- **Deliverables:**
  - `research-lab/research/benchmarks/longmemeval/adapter.py`
  - `research-lab/research/benchmarks/locomo/adapter.py`
  - Unit tests under `research-lab/research/benchmarks/{longmemeval,locomo}/tests/`

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Adapters never mutate raw benchmark files (`MANIFEST.txt` hashes remain intact).
- [ ] `LongMemEval` adapter enforces chronological session sorting and `D-23` reference-time handling.
- [ ] Smoke test with a dummy `B0` responder runs end-to-end through both official scorers.
