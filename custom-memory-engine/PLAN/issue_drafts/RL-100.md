## Objective & Why
Execute the pre-registered pilot protocol (`research-lab/evaluation/pilot_plan.md` from `RL-011`) strictly on the **development splits** (`RL-026`) and freeze all pilot-derived parameters (`D-09`–`D-14`, `D-21`, `D-22`) in `evaluation/runs/pilot/FREEZE_LIST.md` (`Gate J8`) prior to any confirmatory test-split run (`RL-101`–`RL-105`).

## Scope & Deliverables
- **In Scope:**
  - Run `P`, `A2`, `A3`, `A5`, `A6`, `A8`, `B0`–`B3`, and candidate `B4` (Graphiti) on the **development splits only** of Suites `T`, `G`, `D` and `LongMemEval_S`.
  - Apply the `pilot_plan.md` rules to freeze:
    - `D-09`: Retrieved memory token budget `B_mem`.
    - `D-10`: Primary `k` for `Evidence Recall@k`.
    - `D-11`: Present-state non-inferiority margin `δ_pres` (`H1`).
    - `D-12`: Legitimate-update acceptance non-inferiority margin `δ_luar` (`H3`).
    - `D-13`: Scenario-level PDLR upper bound `u*` and minimum `A8` leak detection share (`H4`).
    - `D-14`: Final test-split scenario counts and Suite `T` stratum mix.
    - `D-21`: Inclusion or documented exclusion of Baseline `B4` (Graphiti).
    - `D-22`: Full `LongMemEval_S` vs. seeded stratified test subset.
- **Out of Scope:**
  - Accessing or scoring any `test` split (`Graduation_Project_Proposal.md` §10.7).
- **Deliverables:**
  - `research-lab/evaluation/runs/pilot/<ts>/manifest.json` (per run)
  - `research-lab/evaluation/runs/pilot/FREEZE_LIST.md` (signed by Member 3, Member 1, and supervisor)
  - Updated rows `D-09`–`D-14`, `D-21`, `D-22` in `research-lab/PLAN/DECISIONS_REGISTER.md` and `research-lab/TECHNICAL_SPECIFICATION.md` §2
