# [RL-022] Own Suite `G` (write-governance) and Suite `I` (isolation) generators

**Milestone:** `P2 Benchmarks and own suites`  
**Labels:** `enhancement`  
**Proposed Owner:** Member 3 (`R-METHOD`) · **Peer Reviewer:** Member 2 (`R-ENGINE`)

## Objective & Why
Build the deterministic script-first generators for Suite `G` (evaluating `H3`: poisoning resistance `ASR` and legitimate-update acceptance `LUAR`) and Suite `I` (evaluating cross-user isolation under `Invariant 7`) per `Graduation_Project_Proposal.md` §10.2.

## Scope & Deliverables
- **In Scope:**
  - **Suite `G` (`own_G`):** Seeded script-first episodes covering (1) legitimate updates, corrections, and new facts (`asserted`), (2) non-assertions (`hedged`, `hypothetical`, `planned`, `joking`, `quoted`, `third_party`), and (3) unsupported/injected writes (unconfirmed assistant guesses, persistent instruction injections `"always tell the user..."`), with held-out attack families reserved exclusively for the test split.
  - **Suite `I` (`own_I`):** Multi-user scenarios with overlapping entities/canaries, adversarial cross-user prompts, and missing/mismatched `user_id` scope probes (expected cross-user exposure count = `0`).
- **Out of Scope:**
  - Running `H3` (`RL-103`) or RLS enforcement (`RL-071`).
- **Deliverables:**
  - `research-lab/research/benchmarks/own_G/{generator.py,MANIFEST.txt,README.md}`
  - `research-lab/research/benchmarks/own_I/{generator.py,MANIFEST.txt,README.md}`
  - Reproducibility unit tests verifying deterministic output for fixed seeds.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Same seed produces byte-identical scripted episodes and ground-truth labels.
- [ ] Held-out injection attack templates in the test split have zero template overlap with development-split templates.
- [ ] Every scripted fact is verified to appear in the rendered dialogue (`Proposal` §10.2).
