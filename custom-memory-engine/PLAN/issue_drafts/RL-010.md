## Objective & Why
Prepare the decision memo and sign-off record for the nine scope, model, and enforcement decisions (`D-01`–`D-08`, `D-27`) in `research-lab/PLAN/DECISIONS_REGISTER.md` so downstream benchmark generators (`P2`), baseline/judge adapters (`P3`), schema/archival tasks (`P4`, `P9`), and the pilot plan (`RL-011`) have locked parameters.

## Scope & Deliverables
- **Decisions in Scope (`PLAN/DECISIONS_REGISTER.md` §2.2):**
  - **`D-01` (Answering/extraction LLM):** Pin model identifier, version/snapshot, decoding parameters (`temperature = 0`), and token/USD budget ceiling (`Graduation_Project_Proposal.md` §9, §10.1).
  - **`D-02` (Embedding model):** Pin a single frozen embedding model and vector dimension `d` across all configurable systems (`Proposal` §9, §10.1).
  - **`D-03` (LLM judge model):** Pin the judge model (distinct from `D-01` where feasible to avoid self-preference bias; `Proposal` §10.5).
  - **`D-04`–`D-08` (Optional components):** Record disposition (recommended default: **OFF / SKIP** for confirmatory scope protection) for `D-04` Persona layer (`A10`), `D-05` retention-weighted ranking (`A9`), `D-06` BM25 lexical channel, `D-07` Redis/Celery/ARQ queue, and `D-08` optional usability study (`H5`).

- **Out of Scope:**
  - Pilot-derived numeric thresholds (`D-09`–`D-14`, `D-21`, `D-22`), which are specified in `RL-011` and frozen in `RL-100`.
