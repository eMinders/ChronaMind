## Hygiene rules

- Report every decision honestly: "Supported", "Not supported", or "Inconclusive" per the proposal §4.1 rules.
- Do not pool results across hypotheses to find an "overall supported".
- Do not re-run an experiment with a different seed to flip a decision. A seed-swap is a sensitivity check, not a confirmation of effect size.
- Keep the limitations statement visible and specific per proposal §13 and §10.5.
