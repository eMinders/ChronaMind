# [RL-015] Determine ethics requirements for the optional usability study

**Status:** Ready for determination preparation. **Labels:** `ready`, `decision`, `help wanted`. **Milestone:** P1 Resolve open decisions.

## Goal
Determine whether the optional study in proposal §10.9 will be attempted and what supervisor/university ethics review or approval is required.

## Deliverables and acceptance
Create `research-lab/PLAN/decisions/D-19.md` recording proposed scope, authority consulted, actual response/date, conditions and next action. If no response exists, leave the decision pending; document a skip if that is the actual outcome. Update D-19 and RL-120 only as supported by evidence.

- Distinguish whether the study will be attempted, whether review is required, and whether approval was granted.
- Never infer approval from silence or RL-001.
- No recruitment, participant contact, data collection or study execution before the required approval.
- Obtain independent peer review; the authorized ethics/supervisor body remains the decision authority.

## Prerequisites and dependencies
RL-001 is owner-confirmed Done. No task predecessor blocks preparation. This decision gates only the optional participant study in RL-120, not core application work.

## Out of scope
Recruitment or conducting the study. This task does not grant ethics approval or change the proposal’s ethics position.

## Review and claiming
Proposed reviewer: a contributor other than the author. Read prerequisites, comment `CLAIMED` with handle, start time and deliverable, self-assign, coordinate overlaps and record the claim in `PROJECT_STATUS.md` §3. Submit a PR with the memo/evidence; update the register/status after independent review and acceptance.
