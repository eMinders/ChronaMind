## Objective
Adapters that **do not alter the data** and that invoke the official scorers (LongMemEval `evaluation.py`, LoCoMo evaluator).

## Scope
- Adapter interface: `(system, question, gold) → score` so B0–B4 and P share identical scorer calls.
- Oracle lookup, split loading, tokenization/formatting.
- Smoke run with `B0` on LongMemEval reproduces published trivial-submission metric values to within rounding.

## Out of scope
- The baselines themselves. Owned by `RL-031`.
- The judge. Owned by `RL-033`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Adapters call the official scorer.
- [ ] Smoke test reproduces trivial scores.
- [ ] Data integrity: adapters do not mutate manifests.
