# [RL-105] `H4` confirmatory experiment on Suite `D` test split (run once)

**Milestone:** `P10 Pilot and experiments`  
**Labels:** `enhancement`  
**Proposed Owner:** Member 2 (`R-ENGINE`) · **Peer Reviewer:** Member 3 (`R-METHOD`)

## Objective & Why
Execute the one-shot confirmatory experiment for `H4` (`Graduation_Project_Proposal.md` §§4.1, 10.3, 10.6; `TECHNICAL_SPECIFICATION.md` §9.3) on the frozen Suite `D` test split, evaluating `P` and positive control `A8` (`P-nolineage`) across all 7 in-boundary channels.

## Scope & Deliverables
- **In Scope:**
  - Run `P` and `A8` (`P-nolineage`) once on the frozen Suite `D` test split (`N_D` scenarios).
  - Compute primary **scenario-level `PDLR(P)`** and its exact one-sided 97.5% Clopper–Pearson upper bound (`1 − 0.025^(1/N_D)` at 0 leaks), plus positive-control scenario leak share for `A8`.
  - Compute diagnostics: event-level PDLR, per-channel leak counts across Channels 1–7 (`Proposal` §10.3), never-stated control probe false-match rate, and multi-turn re-learning cases (reported separately).
  - Apply the three-conjunct `Proposal` §4.1 decision rule (`0` leaks for `P`, exact CP upper bound `≤ u*`, and `A8` leak share `≥` frozen `D-13` threshold) to classify `H4` as `Supported`, `Not supported`, or `Inconclusive`.
- **Out of Scope:**
  - Modifying `u*`, `N_D`, or `D-13` after `RL-100` is frozen.
- **Deliverables:**
  - `research-lab/evaluation/runs/<ts>/H4/{manifest.json,answers.jsonl,scores.jsonl,per_cluster.jsonl,report.md}`

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Suite `D` test split is executed **exactly once** under the frozen `RL-100` configuration.
- [ ] `report.md` reports scenario-level `PDLR(P)`, exact Clopper–Pearson 97.5% upper bound, `A8` positive-control leak share, per-channel counts (Channels 1–7), never-stated probe rate, and re-learning counts.
- [ ] `H4` outcome (`Supported` / `Not supported` / `Inconclusive`) is derived mechanically from `TECHNICAL_SPECIFICATION.md` §9.3.
