# [RL-012] Verify PostgreSQL and pgvector compatibility

**Status:** Ready for evidence preparation. **Labels:** `ready`, `decision`, `help wanted`. **Milestone:** P1 Resolve open decisions.

## Goal
Recommend a supported PostgreSQL version and compatible pgvector candidate. PostgreSQL 18.6 and pgvector v0.8.7 are candidates, not accepted pins until verified.

## Deliverables and acceptance
Create `research-lab/PLAN/decisions/D-25.md` comparing current supported stable candidates with dated upstream sources. Cover:

- PostgreSQL support lifecycle and pgvector compatibility/licensing.
- Image/tag/digest and actual build or extension-install evidence.
- Relevant `btree_gist`, range/exclusion, transaction and `SKIP LOCKED` requirements in `PLAN/ARCHITECTURE.md` §7.

Record alternatives, rationale and remaining gaps in the decision register. If a target-specific reduced-schema SQL run is required, save a new log without overwriting historical evidence. Metadata alone does not prove runtime compatibility. An independent reviewer checks the evidence and downstream consequences.

## Prerequisites and dependencies
RL-001 is owner-confirmed Done. No task predecessor blocks evidence preparation. RL-012 blocks RL-005, RL-040 and RL-041. RL-005 additionally requires RL-003/RL-004 and the dedicated-workflow boundary exception. RL-013 is a parallel decision task.

## Out of scope
Full-engine/concurrency validation (RL-060/RL-091), creating CI, installing Lab dependencies, making a lockfile, or starting implementation. Do not change frozen research decisions or the protocol.
