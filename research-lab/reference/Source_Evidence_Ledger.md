**Depth categories used in this ledger** *(v3; made per-row visible in v4)*. Each ledger row carries one of these labels in the new **Reading depth** column of Sections A and B, and nothing stronger:

- *metadata/abstract inspected* — title, authors, venue, abstract read.
- *full text downloaded or retrieved* — PDF or HTML saved; not necessarily read.
- *selected full-text passages read* — specific sections or paragraphs read, logged in Section E.
- *full text read comprehensively* — end-to-end reading of the paper's substantive content.
- *experiments independently reproduced* — the paper's reported experiments were rerun with its artifacts.

Downloading a PDF is **not** described as "paper read". **No paper's experiments were reproduced in V2, V3, or V4.** No ledger row was promoted in V4 beyond what its V3 evidence record supports; the V4 column surfaces the depth that was already implicit in the Section E log and the Scope note.
