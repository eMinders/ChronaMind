## 3. Technical and methodological changes (summary)

- Data model: `MEMORY_VERSION` with two ranges, predicate schema, derivation and evidence tables, deletion epoch, job table with idempotency key, restricted audit schema. Nine invariants, each tied to an enforcement mechanism and a test, with those a constraint cannot express labelled.
- Fast and slow paths: snapshot-consistent retrieval, per-user ordering, dead-letter handling, visibility lag.
- Security: threat model stated; exclusion before context assembly; RLS as a second layer with the PostgreSQL bypass caveats.
- Evaluation: primary endpoints with numerators and denominators; script-first suites with controls; freeze procedure; judge validation; cluster-aware statistics; separate reporting; quality–cost analysis defined by dominance.
